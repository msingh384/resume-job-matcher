🔄 RESTART YOUR BACKEND TO SEE THE NEW PAGE!

In the terminal where your backend is running:

1. Press Ctrl+C to stop it

2. Restart:
   cd /Users/mannanrajsingh/resume-job-matcher
   source venv/bin/activate
   python backend/app.py

3. Open in browser:
   http://localhost:8000

You'll now see a beautiful setup page instead of raw JSON! 🎉
